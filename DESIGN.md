# PetCare Hub Design System

## Overview
Warm, trustworthy, and modern pet wellness and e-commerce experience. Uses emerald and teal botanical accents to evoke animal health, natural nutrition, and medical reliability.

## Colors
- **Primary (#059669 - Emerald 600):** Trust, health, and vitality for active buttons and badges.
- **Secondary (#0d9488 - Teal 600):** Supporting accents, gradients, and secondary highlights.
- **Neutral Dark (#0f172a - Slate 900):** Deep contrast typography and headers.
- **Surface (#ffffff):** Crisp, accessible clean card backgrounds.

## Typography
- Primary family: Inter / system sans-serif.
- Clear legible hierarchy across product cards, dashboard charts, and care reminders.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do provide clean feedback states for asynchronous data loading.
- Do gracefully degrade authenticated widgets when viewed by guest users.
- Don't block core catalog exploration on unauthenticated sessions.
